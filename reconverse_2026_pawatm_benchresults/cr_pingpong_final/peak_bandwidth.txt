Peak bandwidth and saturation
==============================================================================

Bandwidth is size / median one-way time.  'peak GB/s' is the best value
observed and 'at size' where it occurred; 'final step' is the gain over
the last doubling, shown for reference.

The verdict asks WHERE the maximum sits, not whether the last step was
positive -- these curves are not monotonic.  A peak is a LOWER BOUND only
when it sits at the largest size measured AND the curve was still climbing
into it (>2% over the final doubling); then the range is too short.
A maximum reached mid-range and then held or lost is a real peak, even if
the curve ticks up again afterwards.
